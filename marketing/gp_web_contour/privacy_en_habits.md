**NOVAX Habits Privacy Policy**

Last updated: November 2026
App: NOVAX Habits (`com.novax.habits`)
Developer: NOVAX
Contact: [СКРЫТО:EMAIL]

1. **No personal data collection**
   NOVAX Habits does NOT collect, transmit, or store any personal data. All
   habit data, logs, and settings remain exclusively on your device.

2. **No tracking or analytics**
   We do NOT use analytics tools (Firebase, Google Analytics, etc.), tracking
   SDKs, or any third-party data sharing.

3. **Internet use**
   The app works offline. Internet access is used only for Google Play
   billing when you choose to upgrade to the full version. No habit data is
   ever sent over the network.

4. **Local notifications**
   All reminders are scheduled and delivered locally on your device. We do
   not use push notifications or cloud messaging services.

5. **Data storage**
   Your data is stored only in the device's local storage. It is never
   synced to external servers or cloud services.

6. **No account required**
   You do not need to create an account, provide an email, or sign in.

7. **Children's privacy**
   NOVAX Habits does not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

8. **Changes**
   If this policy changes, the update date above will change. Continued use
   of the app means you accept the updated policy.
