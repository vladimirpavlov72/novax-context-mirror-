**NOVAX H2O Privacy Policy**

Last updated: November 2026
App: NOVAX H2O (`com.novax.h2o`)
Developer: NOVAX
Contact: [СКРЫТО:EMAIL]

1. **No personal data collection**
   NOVAX H2O does NOT collect, transmit, or store any personal data. All
   hydration logs, goals, and settings remain exclusively on your device.

2. **No tracking or analytics**
   We do NOT use analytics tools (Firebase, Google Analytics, etc.), tracking
   SDKs, or any third-party data sharing.

3. **Internet use**
   The app works offline. Internet access is used only for Google Play
   billing when you choose to upgrade to the full version. No hydration data
   is ever sent over the network.

4. **Local notifications**
   All drink reminders are scheduled and delivered locally on your device.
   We do not use push notifications or cloud messaging services.

5. **Data storage**
   Your data is stored only in the device's local storage. It is never
   synced to external servers or cloud services.

6. **No account required**
   You do not need to create an account, provide an email, or sign in.

7. **Health disclaimer**
   NOVAX H2O is a hydration diary and reminder tool, not a medical device.
   For health conditions and personal water intake questions, consult your
   doctor.

8. **Children's privacy**
   NOVAX H2O does not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

9. **Changes**
   If this policy changes, the update date above will change. Continued use
   of the app means you accept the updated policy.
