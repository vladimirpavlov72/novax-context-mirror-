**NOVAX QR Privacy Policy**

Last updated: November 2026
App: NOVAX QR (`com.novax.qrscanner`)
Developer: NOVAX
Contact: [СКРЫТО:EMAIL]

1. **No personal data collection**
   NOVAX QR does NOT collect, transmit, or store any personal data. All scan
   history, created codes, and settings remain exclusively on your device.

2. **No tracking or analytics**
   We do NOT use analytics tools (Firebase, Google Analytics, etc.), tracking
   SDKs, or any third-party data sharing.

3. **Offline-first**
   QR and barcodes are recognized directly on your device. The app works
   fully offline; no scan content is ever sent over the network.

4. **Internet use**
   Internet access is used only for Google Play billing when you choose to
   upgrade to the full version. No scan data is ever sent.

5. **Camera and storage permissions**
   Camera access is used only to scan codes. Storage access is used only to
   scan codes from your gallery at your request. We do not access any other
   files or data.

6. **Data storage**
   Your data is stored only in the device's local storage. It is never
   synced to external servers or cloud services.

7. **No account required**
   You do not need to create an account, provide an email, or sign in.

8. **Children's privacy**
   NOVAX QR does not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

9. **Changes**
   If this policy changes, the update date above will change. Continued use
   of the app means you accept the updated policy.
